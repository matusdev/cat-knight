## Full description (both stores, max 4000 characters)

High above the clouds stands the Sky Castle, and the only way there is a road of bridges and islands floating in the air. A fluffy cat knight in steel armour sets off — and jumps all by itself. Your job is to guide it: tilt your phone and the cat flies from island to island.

LAND PRECISELY — BUILD A STREAK
Land in the centre of an island and switch lanes to grow your streak and multiply your coins. Flip mid-air, slash crows on the fly, land on moving and wild islands — daring play pays more. A long streak awakens the Knight's Fury: coins fly to the cat and one mistake is forgiven.

FIVE WORLDS, FIVE BOSSES
Sky Bridges, Frost Peaks, Fire Cliffs, Phantom Ruins and the Dragon Citadel — each world has its own sky, weather and traps. At the end of every world a boss awaits: the Dog Warlord, the Frost and Lava Golems, the Phantom Knight and the Flame Dragon. Deflect their attacks with your sword and send them right back!

TWO MODES
• Endless run: how far can the cat fly? Worlds change along the way and bosses swoop in.
• Castle quest: 35 levels with three stars each. Reach the gate, collect the coins and don't lose a single life.

TRAPS AND WONDERS
Slippery ice, vanishing phantom islands, powder kegs, swinging axes, fire and spikes. Plus mushroom trampolines, shields and a golden feather that gives the cat griffin wings to soar above the road collecting coins.

SHOP AND QUESTS
Change armour, capes and swords. Upgrade wings, magnet, double coins, fury and shield. Complete daily quests and unlock achievements.

BEAUTIFUL AND COMFORTABLE
• Day and night, blizzards, thunderstorms and falling ash across the worlds
• Tilt or touch controls, left-handed layout
• Graphics settings for any phone
• English and Russian
